About Shubham Tech

Technology, equipment and service—connected in one place.

Shubham Tech and IT Solutions is focused on toll infrastructure and IT support. Our approach combines equipment knowledge, practical troubleshooting and service coordination to help toll operations stay organized and operational.

Our role

A technical partner for the details that keep operations running.

Modern toll environments depend on many small systems working together: RFID, AVC, lane controllers, barriers, traffic lights, loops, cameras, printers, intercoms, UPS systems, servers and network equipment.

Our role is to support those building blocks through supply, installation, repair, maintenance and IT assistance. We can work around individual equipment requirements as well as broader lane or plaza-level needs.

Whether the requirement is planned installation or an unexpected fault, the goal remains the same: clear diagnosis, practical action and dependable follow-through.
